## Summary
Add a "Dwarf Heritage Forge" mechanic to the Dungeoncrawler Character Creator. This feature allows players to customise their dwarf characters' heritage through a forge-style interface—selecting ancestral traits, bonus proficiencies, and backstory lore snippets tied to specific dwarven clans or geographical origins.

## Problem statement
Currently, the Dungeoncrawler Character Creator offers generic race/class selection with minimal depth for dwarves. Players have repeatedly requested more flavour and mechanical distinction for dwarf heritage. This feature addresses player demand for cultural depth, flavour choices, and meaningful mechanical bonuses that feel authentic to the Dungeoncrawler world.

## Proposed solution
Introduce a multi-step "Forge" UI flow within the character creator, accessible when a player selects "Dwarf" as their race. Steps:
1. **Choose Clan Origin** – Select from 4–6 dwarven clans (e.g., Ironholds, Ashvein, Stonecrest, Deepmantle, Embervault, Goldpeak), each with unique lore blurbs.
2. **Select Ancestral Traits** – Pick 2 traits from a clan-specific pool (e.g., Stonecunning +2, Forge Resilience, Deep Tremorsense, Runic Aptitude).
3. **Choose a Backstory Lore Snippet** – Select or customise a brief backstory text anchored to their clan/origin.
4. **Confirm Heritage Profile** – Summarised character heritage card shown before saving.

## Acceptance criteria (draft)
- [ ] Dwarf race selection triggers the Heritage Forge flow.
- [ ] At least 4 clans are available with distinct lore blurbs
