## Summary
Players in the Dungeoncrawler game chatbot experience often suggest features, report bugs, or share feedback directly in-game — but currently have no confirmation that their suggestion was received or will be acted on.

This feature adds a **visual notice** to the Dungeoncrawler home page (dungeoncrawler.forseti.life) that:
- Acknowledges that the game collects in-game suggestions and feedback.
- Communicates how suggestions are handled (logged to the project backlog).
- Sets expectations so players feel heard without false promises.

## Problem statement
Players submit suggestions via the in-game chatbot system. There is currently no indication on the home page or in the product UI that:
1. In-game suggestions are captured and reviewed.
2. They feed into an actual development backlog.
3. Players have any agency in the product's development.

This creates a trust gap: players contribute ideas and receive no signal about whether those ideas matter.

## Proposed solution
Add a short, thematic notice section to the Dungeoncrawler home page. The notice should:
- Be styled consistently with the existing dark-fantasy Dungeoncrawler visual design.
- Briefly explain that in-game feedback is collected and reviewed.
- Reference the project backlog (with or without a direct link).
- Be positioned unobtrusively on the home page (e.g., below the hero section, sidebar, or footer-adjacent).
