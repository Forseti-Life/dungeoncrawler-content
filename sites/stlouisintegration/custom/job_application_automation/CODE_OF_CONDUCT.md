# Code of Conduct

We are committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive community.

## Expected Behavior
- Be respectful and professional
- Welcome diverse perspectives
- Give constructive feedback
- Focus on the community's benefit

## Unacceptable Behavior
- Harassment or discrimination
- Offensive comments
- Personal attacks
- Trolling

## Enforcement

Violations may result in removal from the community.

Report concerns to: conduct@example.com
